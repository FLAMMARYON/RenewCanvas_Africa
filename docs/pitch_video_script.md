# RenewCanvas Africa - 2-Minute Pitch Video Script

## Overview
- **Total Duration:** 2 minutes (120 seconds)
- **Format:** Problem → Solution → Demo → Impact → Ask
- **Tone:** Passionate, clear, professional

---

## SCRIPT WITH TIMING

### OPENING - The Problem (0:00 - 0:20)
**[Show: B-roll of plastic waste in African cities, or stock footage]**

> "Across Africa, we're drowning in plastic waste. In Rwanda alone, thousands of tons of recyclable materials end up in landfills every year. At the same time, talented African artists struggle to find markets, materials, and sustainable income."

> "What if we could solve both problems at once?"

---

### THE SOLUTION (0:20 - 0:40)
**[Show: RenewCanvas logo, then transition to you speaking to camera]**

> "I'm Nicholas Eke, founder of RenewCanvas Africa — a circular economy platform that transforms waste into art."

> "We collect recyclable materials — plastics, fabrics, metals — clean and sort them, then distribute them to curated artists who create stunning upcycled artwork. Every piece comes with a verified environmental impact story."

---

### LIVE DEMO (0:40 - 1:30)
**[Screen recording of the live site: www.renewcanvas.page]**

> "Let me show you how it works."

**[Navigate through the site while narrating:]**

1. **Homepage (5 sec)**
   > "This is RenewCanvas Africa — live at renewcanvas.page"

2. **How It Works page (10 sec)**
   > "Our four-step process: We collect and clean materials, artists create, we track impact, and buyers support the movement."

3. **Marketplace (15 sec)**
   > "Buyers browse our curated marketplace of upcycled artwork — paintings, sculptures, jewelry, home décor — each with verified impact data."

4. **Artist Dashboard (10 sec)**
   > "Artists get a full dashboard to manage their portfolio, track orders, and even get AI-powered pricing recommendations."

5. **Impact Page (10 sec)**
   > "Every transaction is tracked — kilograms of waste diverted, CO2 avoided, artist income generated. Full transparency."

6. **Virtual Gallery (5 sec)**
   > "Buyers can even preview artwork in our 3D virtual gallery before purchasing."

## TIPS FOR RECORDING

### Technical
- Record screen at 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Use a clean browser (no bookmarks bar, no extensions visible)
- Clear browser history (no embarrassing autocomplete)
- Use incognito mode for clean look
- Ensure good internet connection (site loads fast)

### Speaking
- Speak clearly and with energy
- Smile — passion is contagious
- Practice 2-3 times before recording
- Keep a steady pace (not too fast)

### Production
- Good lighting on your face
- Clean background
- External microphone if possible
- Record in a quiet space
